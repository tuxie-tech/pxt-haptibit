> HaptiBit and TuxieTech were featured at the BBC micro:bit 10th anniversary event at Edgar Street, Hereford, where around 200 people used micro:bits to create a giant digital wave. For the current documentation, see the [main HaptiBit page](../).

To mark ten years of the BBC micro:bit, a crowd of around 200 people gathered at Hereford FC's Edgar Street ground, each holding a micro:bit. Using the radio built into every board, they passed a signal from one to the next to create a giant digital wave rippling around the stand. HaptiBit and TuxieTech were part of the day, and the event was picked up by local and national news outlets.

Here is some of the coverage so far. We'll add more here as it appears, including a page from the micro:bit Foundation.
